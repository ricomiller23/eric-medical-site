💉 DARATUMUMAB (Darzalex) - Anti-CD38 Monoclonal Antibody

Mechanism: Binds CD38 on myeloma cells, triggering immune-mediated cell death (CDC, ADCC, ADCP)

How It Addresses YOUR 1q21 Gain: Direct killing independent of genetic abnormalities. Immune-mediated attack overwhelms Mcl-1 overexpression.

How It Addresses YOUR TP53: Immune killing doesn't require p53 function.

Clinical Data: D-RVd achieves 54.8% MRD-negative (vs 32.4% RVd alone)

💊 LENALIDOMIDE (Revlimid) - Cereblon E3 Ligase Modulator

Mechanism: "Molecular Glue" - Forms CRL4^CRBN E3 ligase, recruits IKZF1/IKZF3 for degradation

How It Addresses YOUR 1q21: IKZF1/3 degradation suppresses CKS1B & MCL1 expression

Synergy with Daratumumab: Enhances ADCC through NK cell activation

💉 BORTEZOMIB (Velcade) - Proteasome Inhibitor

Why ESSENTIAL for YOUR Disease:

  • MCL1 on 1q21 is amplified → Bortezomib's NOXA specifically counteracts this
  • TP53 stabilization → Even with 3 copies, can trigger apoptosis

⚠️ Side Effect Alert: Peripheral Neuropathy (40-60% risk)

💊 DEXAMETHASONE - Synthetic Glucocorticoid

Mechanism: NF-κB transrepression → Direct apoptosis

Why It Matters for YOUR 1q21: NF-κB is constitutively activated by 1q21 genes. Dexamethasone directly blocks this.

Dosing: 40 mg PO weekly
